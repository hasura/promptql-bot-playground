Connect Higgsfield once

Checking…
  1. Get a key. Sign in at open.higgsfield.ai/api-keys, create an API key and press Copy API Key. (A new Higgsfield account needs credits — a full render is about $17.)
  2. Add it in PromptQL. Open My Data in PromptQL → Higgsfield (user private) → Connect → paste exactly what you copied into the one secure field. Don't split it or add a prefix.
  3. Come back here. Reopen this app and accept the permission prompt so it can render on your behalf. Check again.

Just added or changed your API key? This app only picks it up after a reload — hit refresh in the artifact heading (top of this panel), then check the status above again.

HOW IT WORKS

One recipe, run exactly as it was the first time.

THE RECIPE

Higgsfield's Genjutsu motion transfer, at 720p, with your two photos as Image 1 and Image 2 and this exact prompt:

keep Everything the same in the video just replace the faces with the two people in the 2 images provided.

The model keeps the motion, camera and timing and rebuilds the people from your photos. The finished picture gets the clip's original soundtrack laid back under it so the lip and beat sync are exact.

WHAT MAKES IT GOOD

The photos do almost all the work. Use a front-on, sharp, well-lit shot with a neutral expression and hair visible — a phone selfie in daylight is ideal. Tilted, smiling or low-res cutouts produce weak profiles.

The prompt says "replace the faces", so hair from the source performers can carry over. Two renders won't be identical: there's no seed, so a second attempt is a genuine reroll.
